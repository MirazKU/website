Biography & Background

Bridging Historical Inquiry with Frontline Reporting

Md. Mirazul Islam is a dedicated journalist and researcher based in Khulna, Bangladesh. He holds a Bachelor of Arts (Honours) in History and Civilization from Khulna University (2025), where he cultivated a disciplined approach to archival inquiry, oral history, and critical content analysis.

Alongside his academic research, Mirazul has built an extensive track record in journalism across national and regional media outlets. He currently serves as Khulna University Correspondent for Daily Kaler Kantho, Contributor for Daily Observer, and Contributor for Bangladesh Guardian. From 2022 to 2026, he served in key executive leadership positions at the Khulna University Journalists' Association (KUJA), advancing from Associate Member to General Secretary and Executive Member.
