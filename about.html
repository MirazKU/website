Biography & Academic Profile

Md. Mirazul Islam is an investigative journalist and researcher based in Khulna, Bangladesh. He is currently pursuing an M.A. in International History (Expected 2026) and holds a B.A. in History and Civilization (2025) from Khulna University.

In active journalism, Mirazul currently serves as Reporter for Daily Kaler Kantho (November 2024 – Present), producing over 300+ special reports on corruption, investigative research, institutional accountability, and university governance. He also contributes as Feature Writer for Daily Agamir Somoy (May 2025 – Present), producing in-depth narratives on human rights and untold social issues. Previously, he served as Campus Contributor for the Daily Observer (April 2024 – December 2025) and Campus Correspondent for Daily Bangladesh (March 2022 – November 2024).

Demonstrating sustained newsroom leadership, he served as General Secretary, Executive Member, and Member of the Khulna University Journalists' Association (KUJA) from February 2022 to October 2026. He is also a Founding Member of the Khulna University Research Society (KURS).
