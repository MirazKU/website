Profession in Peril: Adaptive Strategies among Coastal Fishermen in the Sundarbans

Climate Vulnerability, Mangrove Delta Ecology & Livelihood Adaptation

An empirical field thesis investigating how escalating salinity, unpredictable weather extremes, and mangrove ecological restrictions affect the traditional socio-economic coping mechanisms of coastal fishermen in the Sundarbans delta.

Methodology: Qualitative Field Inquiries & Oral History Interviews
Institution: History and Civilization Discipline, Khulna University
Focus Area: Sundarbans Mangrove Fringe & Coastal Communities

Heritage Date Molasses: International Market Expansion & Sarojganj Trade Inquiries

Agricultural Economics & Agro-Heritage Export Potential

An in-depth empirical inquiry into Chuadanga's 300-year-old Sarojganj date palm jaggery trade, investigating export barriers, chemical purity benchmarks, cold chain preservation, and potential international market access.

Lead Researcher: Dr. Md. Mahmud Alam (Associate Prof., Khulna University)
Collaborator: Kazi Md. Mostafa Kamal (Dhaka University)

Environmental Survey Sep 2025

Mayur River Encroachment, Severe Industrial Pollution & Ecological Devastation

Environmental Economics & Urban Hydrology

Comprehensive water quality analysis and economic impact assessment evaluating alarming dissolved oxygen depletion (under 1 mg/L), illegal industrial encroachment, and catastrophic aquatic biodiversity loss in Khulna's vital Mayur River basin.

Lead Researcher: Prof. Dr. Mohammad Ziaul Haider (Economics Discipline, KU)
Key Findings: Most polluted river in southwestern Bangladesh; severe DO deficit
Publication: Daily Kaler Kantho (Special Investigation)
